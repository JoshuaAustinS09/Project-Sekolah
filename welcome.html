SMA Kolese Loyola

Men and Women for Others

Competence

Ability to effectively apply knowledge, skills, and attitudes to perform tasks or solve problems successfully.

Conscience

Inner sense that helps distinguish between right and wrong and guides their actions accordingly.

Compassion

Feeling of deep empathy and concern for others, accompanied by a desire to help alleviate their suffering.

Commitment

Dedication and responsibility to consistently pursue a goal or uphold a promise despite challenges.

SMA Kolese Loyola adalah sekolah menengah atas swasta Katolik di Semarang, Jawa Tengah, yang didirikan pada tanggal 3 Agustus 1949. Sekolah ini didirikan pada masa pasca-perang kemerdekaan Indonesia di Semarang oleh Pater Jan van Waayenburg, SJ
